# Inventories

An inventory makes what a person or community can share easier to find. It is a catalog of
things, separate from help requests and skills.

## Private until shared

A personal item belongs to one member. It is visible only to that owner until they choose
communities to share it with. Both the owner and viewer must currently be active members of a
shared community. Leaving withdraws visibility; the saved sharing choices return on rejoining.

A community-owned item belongs to the community. Its active admins manage it, and active
members can see it when available. The member who added it is its creator, not its owner.

## Availability

An unavailable item stays in its manager's inventory but is hidden from other members.
For a personal item, the manager is its owner. For community property, managers are active admins.
Authorization follows current membership, so an old login token cannot preserve removed access.

## A catalog first

The first release supports listing, editing, sharing and browsing things. It does not track
loans or returns, add photos, or change skill-based matching. Directed borrowing is a later
part of the inventories rollout described in ADR-099.

See [Sharing Your Things](/docs/guides/sharing-your-things-guide) for the workflow.
