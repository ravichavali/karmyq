# Skill Endorsements: Vouching for What You Saw

When someone helps you, or you help them, you learn something real about what they can do. A
**vouch** records that: you confirm one of their skills, and the trust between the two of you grows.

---

## Who Can Vouch

You can vouch for a skill when both of these are true:

- **You completed an exchange together.** It doesn't matter who asked and who helped. The exchange
  must be completed, not just matched.
- **You can see the skill.** You share an active community with the person and they share their
  skills (the default). See [Who can see your skills](/docs/guides/profile-guide).

You can't vouch for your own skills. A vouch stays valid if you later leave the community: the
exchange happened, so the vouch remains true.

## Where to Vouch

The quickest place is the completed request. The **Exchange completed** notification opens it, and
it shows each person you completed that exchange with, a private rating, and their skills with a
**Vouch** button beside each one you can vouch for. Only the two people in an exchange see each other
there: the person who asked sees each helper they completed it with, and a helper sees only the person
who asked. Other members who can read the request, a helper whose offer didn't go ahead, and a
preferred provider see no one.

You can also open the **Helping** tab and tap the name on a completed exchange; the card shows the
same skills and buttons. Or vouch from a member's skills in your community's **People** tab.

Each skill shows how many people currently vouch for it.

## Changing Your Mind

Tap **Withdraw vouch** to take it back. The count drops at once and the trust it added goes away.
You can vouch again later.

If the person deletes the skill, its vouches go with it. If they turn off skill sharing, the
vouches stay but nobody else can see them until sharing is back on.

## How Vouches Feed Trust

A vouch adds to the **trust connection** between the two of you in the community where your
exchange happened. Only *whether* you vouched counts there, not how many skills you vouched for:

| What happened | Trust added for the pair |
|---|---|
| Nobody vouched | none |
| One of you vouched (for one skill or five) | one vouch |
| You both vouched for each other | two vouches |

The skill counts keep the detail; the trust graph records mutual confidence. Karmyq recomputes
this from the vouches themselves every time one changes, so retries, withdrawals and deleted
skills always land on the right number. A vouch never counts twice.
