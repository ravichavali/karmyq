# Joining Karmyq

There are two ways into Karmyq — an invitation from someone already here, or open registration.
Both end the same way: with you standing in your first community, looking at the beginnings of
your own web.

## The invited path (the best way in)

If someone sent you an invite link, open it. You'll land on their invitation itself: who invited
you, and which community they're bringing you into. Your account form lives right inside that
context — fill it in and you join their community directly, no browsing required.

Your inviter matters here. The moment you accept, Karmyq remembers the **invitation bond** between
you — the relationship that brought you in. It isn't trust yet: trust on Karmyq is earned through
real exchanges, never granted by a link. But it's where your story starts, and it becomes trust
the first time you help each other.

## The open path

No invitation? Register from the sign-up page, then pick a community to join from the discovery
list. You can search by location or interests. Your first join works exactly like an invited
arrival — minus the inviter.

## The arrival moment

After your first join — either path — you land on a welcome screen showing your **first belonging
graph**: you, placed on the ring of the community you just entered, among your new neighbours.

- On the invited path, a dashed line connects you to your inviter — that's your invitation bond,
  drawn differently from trust bonds because it hasn't been earned yet. Every exchange between
  you makes it real.
- On the open path, you'll see yourself among your neighbours with no bonds yet. That's not
  empty — it's the honest starting picture. Bonds appear as you give and receive help.

One button takes you to the community's open asks — the fastest way to form your first real bond
is to answer one. You can also skip; the welcome only shows once either way.

## Watching your web grow (and fade)

Your network view (My Network) tells the rest of the story. Newly formed bonds carry a bright
"New bond" emphasis for about a month. Established bonds show as strong or warm. Bonds you
haven't tended fade — first visibly softer, then nearly forgotten, and eventually Karmyq lets the
detail go entirely (see *Your Memory and Relationships*). Helping each other again at any point
keeps a bond alive.

That's the whole loop: an invitation or a join brings you in, exchanges turn presence into trust,
and the graph never claims more than what actually happened.
