# Managing Your Notifications

Karmyq's notification system is split into two streams: **community** and **provider**. This separation means providers aren't distracted by unrelated community pings while doing provider work, and community members aren't confused by provider-only alerts.

---

## The Two Notification Bells

If you have a provider profile, you'll see two notification indicators in the top nav:

| Indicator | Color | Stream |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| Bell icon | Red badge | Community notifications |
| Briefcase icon | Amber badge | Provider notifications |

If you don't have a provider profile, only the community bell is shown.

---

## Community Notifications

These appear in the red bell and cover mutual aid activity:

| Type | When it fires |
|------|---------------|
| **Match offer** | Someone offered to help with your request |
| **Match completed** | A match was marked fulfilled |
| **Karma awarded** | You received karma points |
| **Karma milestone** | You hit a karma milestone (10, 50, 100, 250, 500, 1000) |
| **New request** | A community member posted a new help request |
| **Join request** | Someone asked to join your community (admins only) |

---

## Provider Notifications

These appear in the amber briefcase and cover service provider activity:

| Type | When it fires |
|------|---------------|
| **Pre-selected** | A requester selected you as their preferred provider |
| **Request matched** | A new request matching your service type was posted |
| **Review received** | A client left a review on your provider profile |
| **Commitment reminder** | It's time to head to a scheduled pickup or session |

Provider notifications only fire when they require your attention — you won't get a notification every time any request is posted, only those that match your service type.

---

## Opening a Notification Stream

Click the bell or briefcase to open a dropdown showing that stream's recent notifications. Each notification shows:
- Title and summary
- How long ago it was received
- A **call-to-action button** linking to the relevant page

Click **Mark all read** to clear the unread badge for that stream.

---

## Volume Philosophy

Karmyq is intentionally quiet. Notifications only fire when they are action-driving: a match proposed, a review left, a milestone hit. You won't be pinged for passive events (profile views, feed impressions, generic community activity). The goal is that every notification you see is something worth responding to.
