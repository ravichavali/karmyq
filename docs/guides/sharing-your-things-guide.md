# Sharing Your Things

Keep a list of things you could lend, such as a ladder, a book or a camping kit.
Open **My things** from the menu. Your new items are **private until you share them**.

## Add and manage an item

Choose **Add item**, give it a name, choose a category and optionally describe its condition.
Save it to your personal list. Use **Edit** to update it, or **Delete** and confirm to remove it.

Choose **Share** beside an item, select the communities that may see it, then **Save sharing**.
Only communities where you are an active member are valid choices. Uncheck every community to
make the item private again. Other members do not see your private items.

## Availability and membership

Choose **Mark unavailable** when you cannot share an item. Only you can see it while unavailable;
your community selections stay saved. **Mark available** restores visibility to those communities.
Leaving a community hides your shared items there. Rejoining makes the saved shares visible again.

## Browse shared things

A community's **Shared things** tab has two sections: **Community-owned things** and
**Shared by members**. Active members see available items. Active admins manage community-owned
items and can also see unavailable community property.

This catalog does not track loans, due dates or returns. Adding an item does not create a help request.
