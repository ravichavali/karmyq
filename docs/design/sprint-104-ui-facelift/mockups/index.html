Sprint 104 · UI Facelift Research

Three directions to finish the warm system

All three keep the locked palette (earthy green / teal / cream / brown / orange) and type (Fraunces + Hanken Grotesk) and satisfy the non-negotiable principles P1–P7 (one reading column, border-not-shadow cards, one radius, smooth serif ramp, color-from-tokens, quiet density, warm finite states). They differ only on P8 — texture / character. See visual-research.md and recommendations.md.

Direction A · low risk

Tidy Commons

Convergence only. Every surface adopts the community page's existing .kq-* language. No new aesthetic — the facelift is "the fossils stop existing." Consistency without new character.

Field Guide

Convergence + one warm step: a whisper of paper grain, a smooth Fraunces ramp, and a recurring seed/leaf motif. A printed neighbourhood field guide. Genuine character, still inside the shipped aesthetic, all behind droppable tokens.

Almanac
