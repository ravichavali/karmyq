## Objective

Create landing page content that explains Karmyq's trust-based approach to mutual aid, making it clear how we're different from transactional platforms and why that matters.

## Core Narrative Arc

1. **Problem**: Modern platforms destroy trust (zero-trust design, surveillance, transactions)
2. **Insight**: Trust is relational, not transactional - built through connections
3. **Solution**: Karmyq builds trust scaffolding via referral chains and community
4. **Outcome**: Better interactions, stronger communities, restored social fabric

### Section 2: How Trust Works on Karmyq

**Headline**: "Trust isn't a score. It's a path."

**Interactive Demo** (if possible, otherwise visual diagram):

**Scenario**: Maria needs help moving a couch

**Step 1: Show the Request**
```
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│ Help moving couch               │
│ Saturday 2pm, 30 min            │
│ Maria Garcia                    │
│                                 │
│ [Trust Path Badge]              │
│ 2° connection via Sarah         │
│                                 │
│ You → Sarah → Maria             │
│                                 │
│ Sarah can vouch that Maria is   │
│ reliable and respectful.        │
└─────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Step 2: Explain the Trust Path**
> **You're connected through Sarah.**
> - Sarah invited Maria to the community
> - Sarah vouched for Maria's character
> - If there's a problem, Sarah is involved
> - Maria's reputation reflects on Sarah
>
> **This is accountability through relationships, not algorithms.**

**Step 3: Show the Choice**
```
✅ Help Maria (close connection)
   Earn 12 karma (+2 bonus for helping outside your immediate circle)

⏭️  Pass (no judgment)
   Maybe someone closer to Maria will help
```

**Key Points** (callout boxes):

1. **Trust Paths are Transparent**
   - You see exactly how you're connected
   - No black-box algorithms
   - No hidden "trust scores" manipulating you

2. **Trust Grows with Experience**
   - After helping Maria, your connection strengthens
   - Next time, you'll trust her more
   - She'll trust you more
   - Real relationships form

3. **You Control Your Boundaries**
   - Set how far out you're comfortable (1-6 degrees)
   - Help only people you trust
   - Or expand your circle and earn bonus karma
   - Your choice, always

### Section 4: Why This Matters (The Big Picture)

**Headline**: "We're rebuilding the social fabric, one community at a time"

**The Deeper Why**:

> Modern life is lonely. We've lost the interdependence that once bound communities together. We've outsourced care to markets and bureaucracies. We've learned not to trust our neighbors.
>
> **But humans aren't wired for isolation. We're wired for cooperation.**
>
> For 99% of human history, we survived by helping each other. Gift economies, mutual aid, reciprocity - these weren't just nice ideas, they were survival strategies.
>
> **Then we scaled. And we simplified.**
>
> Money became the universal language of cooperation. Markets replaced relationships. Efficiency trumped connection. And we lost something precious: **the experience of being needed and being able to help.**

**Three Statistics** (with visual icons):

```
📊 Loneliness epidemic
   61% of young adults report chronic loneliness
   (Harvard Study, 2020)

🏠 Neighbor disconnection
   Less than 30% of Americans know most of their neighbors
   (Pew Research, 2021)

🤝 Trust collapse
   Trust in institutions, neighbors, and strangers
   at all-time lows across developed nations
   (World Values Survey, 2022)
```

**The Opportunity**:

> **Technology got us into this mess. Technology can help get us out.**
>
> Not by replacing human connection - but by supporting it. Not by creating new markets - but by enabling new forms of cooperation. Not by optimizing transactions - but by facilitating relationships.
>
> **Karmyq is infrastructure for trust.**

### Section 6: The Trust System (For the Curious)

**Headline**: "How we measure good things without destroying them"

**Expandable/Collapsible Sections** (for those who want details):

**▼ Karma: Contribution, Not Worthiness**
```
What It Is:
- Points you earn by helping and being helped
- Decays over 6 months (recent activity matters)
- Community-specific (you might be high-karma in one community, new in another)

What It's NOT:
- Not a credit score (doesn't lock you out of anything)
- Not a competition (no leaderboards pressuring you)
- Not a social hierarchy (everyone starts fresh)

Why It Works:
- Recognizes active contributors
- Prevents karma hoarding (use it or lose it)
- Reflects current engagement, not lifetime achievement
```

**▼ Trust Paths: Connection, Not Surveillance**
```
What It Is:
- The social distance between you and someone else
- 1° = direct connection (you referred them or vice versa)
- 2° = friend of friend
- 3° = extended network
- 4-6° = distant connections

What You Can Do:
- Filter feed by trust distance (only see 1-3° if you prefer)
- Earn bonus karma for helping distant connections
- See exactly who connects you to someone

What It's NOT:
- Not a black-box algorithm deciding who you see
- Not surveillance (we don't track your behavior to manipulate you)
- Not permanent (trust can strengthen or weaken with experience)
```

**▼ Community Health: Emergent, Not Mandated**
```
What We Measure:
- Match completion rate (are requests getting fulfilled?)
- Reciprocity index (do people both ask AND offer?)
- Trust expansion rate (are people helping beyond their comfort zone?)
- Retention (do members stay active?)

What We DON'T Measure:
- Time-on-platform (we're not optimizing for engagement)
- Click-through rates (not an ad platform)
- Viral growth (slow, healthy growth is fine)

Why This Matters:
- Transparent metrics you can see
- Communities can self-correct
- No hidden optimization for platform profit
```

**Key Philosophy** (callout):
> **We assume you're good until proven otherwise.**
>
> Most platforms assume you're bad until proven good. Background checks. Credit holds. Constant surveillance. This creates the very untrustworthiness it claims to prevent.
>
> Karmyq starts with trust and builds scaffolding to protect it - not walls to contain you.
>
> See [Trust-First Design Philosophy](link to ADR-020)

### Section 8: Frequently Asked Questions

**Is this just another gig economy app?**
> No. Gig economy apps commodify human labor. Karmyq facilitates mutual aid. There's no money, no ratings pressure, no algorithmic management. Just people helping people.

**What if someone takes advantage?**
> Communities have multiple safeguards: referral chains create accountability, karma tracks contribution, trust paths show connections, and restorative justice processes handle conflicts. But fundamentally, we design for the 99% who want to help, not defend against the 1% who don't.

**How do you make money?**
> We don't yet. Karmyq is currently funded by [grants/donations/founders]. Future revenue might come from premium community features (analytics, custom integrations) but the core platform will always be free for mutual aid.

**What if my community doesn't exist yet?**
> Start one! Invite 5-7 trusted friends, set your norms, and grow from there. Or join an existing community to see how it works first.

**Is my data private?**
> Yes. We don't sell data. We don't track you across the web. Your trust scores are private (only you see them). Your request/offer history is private (only visible to matches). Your referral chain is yours to share or hide. See our [Privacy Policy](#).

**Can I use this for commercial purposes?**
> Karmyq is designed for mutual aid, not commerce. If money changes hands, use another platform. If you're trading skills, time, or favors - you're in the right place.

**What if I don't have anything to offer?**
> Everyone has something. Time, skills, knowledge, presence. And asking for help is itself a contribution - you give others the gift of being helpful. Start by asking, see what happens.

## Success Metrics for Landing Page

1. **Comprehension**: Do visitors understand what Karmyq is?
   - Survey: "In your own words, what is Karmyq?" (target: 70% accuracy)

2. **Trust Perception**: Do they understand the trust system?
   - Survey: "How does Karmyq differ from other platforms?" (mentions trust/referrals/community)

3. **Conversion**: Do they take action?
   - % who click "Find Community" or "Start Community"
   - % who complete signup flow

4. **Retention**: Do they come back?
   - % who visit landing page and then use the app within 7 days
   - % still active after 30 days

5. **Word of Mouth**: Do they tell others?
   - Referral rate (invites sent per new member)
   - Organic traffic growth
